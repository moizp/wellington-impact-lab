# Fixtures — not the benchmark

Tiny invented policy corpus and 24 + 8 example rows, used only to test the harness and to make
`python -m eval.run_eval` runnable before the real data exists. They are **not** human-verified,
**not** stratified, and far too small for any claim. The real frozen test set, policy corpus and
splits are Stage 1 deliverables in `docs/paper_plan.md` (section 4).
